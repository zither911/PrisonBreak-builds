# Prison Break

**[Download the latest build](https://github.com/zither911/PrisonBreak-builds/releases/latest)** —
Windows 64-bit. Unzip anywhere and run `PrisonBreak.exe`. Nothing to install.

---

You are in a cell. In three days you are somewhere else, or you are not.

Fifty prisons, each one bigger and better watched than the last. The day runs to
a schedule — locked up, out in the yard, locked up again — and the schedule is
the puzzle. The cell doors open for eight seconds at the top of yard time and
shut again whether you are through them or not.

Everything you need is somewhere in the building. A shovel in the yard. A key on
a guard's belt, or buried under the grass where the towers can see you digging
it up. A club, taken from behind. A lockpick and a lock with five pins in it.
Later, a sidearm off the one officer who carries one — if you can get him
looking the other way.

Everything watching you is somewhere too. Guards who walk a route and notice
anyone at their shoulder. Cameras sweeping the halls. Drones overhead that look
straight down, so a roof is the only thing that hides you. Riflemen in the
towers who shout once before they fire. And a control room, deep in the block,
with a switch that turns every one of them off — if you can reach it.

Get it wrong and they come for you. Get to somewhere you are allowed to be
before they arrive, and you are just a prisoner in his cell.

Switch the watchers off and they are no longer only blind — a dead camera and
a grounded drone are a board each, and the two of them together, on your own
bunk, are a charge. Put it on a door, or on the outside wall, and stand back.

Then dig, or unlock, or blow your way out — and in the last ten prisons, down
through a hatch into the service tunnels, past the beams, and up a ladder on the
far side where something is waiting with its rotors running.

**Bring a friend.** Two of you, one shovel, one key. If one is caught the other
can still get him out of solitary — the guard on its door has the only key.

### Reporting a bug

There is a **Report a bug** button in Settings. It sends the level and its seed,
a screenshot and the log along with whatever you type, which is usually the
difference between a bug that gets fixed and one nobody can reproduce.

### Updates

The game checks for a new version when it starts and offers to install it. Press
the button; it downloads, closes, replaces itself and opens again. Your progress
and settings are untouched — they live outside the game folder.

### Windows will warn you

The build is not code signed, so Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"* the
first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Updates after that install
without asking again.

---

This repository holds the builds and nothing else. It is public so the game can
check for updates and you can download one without an account, a token or a
login — the game itself carries no credentials of any kind.
